Choosing

Which mattress is the Signature?

The Prime Aura: 7″ and medium, with layers of 22, 25 and 30 kg/m³ density. Its specially developed 3-zone structure withstands body pressure and weight, to promote proper spinal alignment.

Which is the thickest, and which the thinnest?

The Camellia Euro Top comes up to 12″. The Hybrid Eco is 4″, and the Hybrid Plush 4.5″.

Can I see what is inside before I buy?

Yes. In the 3D showroom you can turn each mattress, press it, and unveil its layers in order, with their densities.

Inside the mattress

What is rebond?

A support layer of foam chips bonded together, at 80 kg/m³ density. It is in the core of both Camellias, the Imperio, the Hybrid Plush and the Vento.

Which mattresses have memory foam?

Both Camellias. The Euro Top and the Pillow Top each have a memory foam layer of 35 kg/m³ density.

What is a profile layer?

A foam layer with a cut, shaped surface. On the Imperio it is 25 kg/m³ and sits on top, for back support and air flow. The Hybrid Plush has a profile cut foam layer on top too.

Covers

What are the covers made of?

Camellia Euro Top: Belgian Tencel, made from eucalyptus yarns, at 300 GSM. Camellia Pillow Top, Prime Aura and Emma French Top: Belgian knitted jacquard. Imperio: knitted jacquard made with Italian technology, in white or blue. Hybrid Plush: Italian knitted jacquard. Hybrid Eco and Vento: removable polyester. Pillows: Turkish knitted jacquard.
